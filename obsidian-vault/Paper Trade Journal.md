# Paper Trade Journal

[[Strategy Index]] · [[Codex Research Protocol]]

Closed terminal paper trades are retained in the server SQLite ledger (up to 200 fills). Export the current Markdown report to preserve them in this vault.

For each research cycle, record:

- Exchange, market, symbol, timeframe, and data source.
- Entry rationale, sampled fill price, costs, exit reason, and net P&L.
- Which hypothesis changed and what the holdout said.
- What was learned and what remains uncertain.

Paper portfolios are server-persistent. A one-time link code lets signed Discord commands access the matching workspace. This simulated ledger is not an exchange account ledger.
